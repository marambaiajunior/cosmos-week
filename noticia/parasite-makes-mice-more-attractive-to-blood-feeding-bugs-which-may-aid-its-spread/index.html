Some parasites, such as Trypanosoma rangeli and Trypanosoma cruzi, can be passed between mammals and blood-feeding insects called triatomine bugs. This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies.

We measured the ability of these mice to prey on nymphs that attempted to feed, because if predation rates increased along with attractiveness, there would be no fitness gain for the parasite," explained Guarneri. This assay showed a lower predation rate for infected mice and transmission rates of nearly 50% among the insects that survived feeding.

Based on these transmission rates, it is possible to suggest that an increase in the number of insects approaching the host would also increase the number of individuals acquiring the infection, since the defensive behavior of the mice is reduced by infection. Among bugs that fed on infected mice, 46.5% acquired the parasite.
