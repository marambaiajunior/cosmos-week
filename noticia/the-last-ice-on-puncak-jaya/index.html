An aerial survey planned for December should yield an estimate of how much ice remains in 2026, Permana said. Measurements at the primary monitoring site on the East Northwall Firn showed a drop from 32 meters (105 feet) thick in 2010 to 4 meters (13 feet) in 2023, Permana said.
