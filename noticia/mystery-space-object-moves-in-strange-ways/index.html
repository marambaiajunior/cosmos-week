1998 KY26 is a mystery space object that accelerates in ways that gravity alone can't explain. A new study suggests light photons from the sun are the cause.

1998 KY26 is an unusual object that orbits the sun between Earth and Mars. Photons of light from the sun might explain the strange movements of 1998 KY26, a new study suggests.

Our beautiful blue and silver poster shows every day’s moon phase for 2027. And as for its strange movements, a new study suggests photons from the sun might be the key.

Korbin Hansen and Masatoshi Hirabayashi at the Georgia Institute of Technology published their new peer-reviewed paper in Icarus on October 28, 2026. Alexander Nazaryan wrote about the latest findings in The New York Times on November 16, 2026.

Near-Earth Objects (NEOs) like 1998 KY26 are difficult to study, even though they are relatively close. In 2025, another paper showed that the object was actually only 1/3 the size that scientists thought it was.

Davide Farnocchia, a navigation engineer at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena, California, came up with an intriguing idea.
