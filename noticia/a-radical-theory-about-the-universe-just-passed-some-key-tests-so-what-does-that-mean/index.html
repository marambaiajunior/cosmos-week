An alternative model in which the Universe is 26.7 billion years old and needs neither dark matter nor dark energy has held up against several major cosmology tests, according to a new University of Ottawa study. By the 1970s, scientists largely accepted the theory that the Universe was permeated by a dark, invisible mass that prevented galaxies from flying apart.

By the 1990s, deep field observations made by the venerable Hubble Space Telescope led scientists to theorize that there was a powerful cosmic force driving expansion, what would come to be known as " Dark Energy. The cosmic Dark Ages refer to the first 100 billion years after the Big Bang and before the formation of the first stars and galaxies.
