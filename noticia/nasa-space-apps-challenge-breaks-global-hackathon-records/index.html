The annual hackathon invites participants to use NASA’s open data to address one of 14 challenges focused on space exploration or Earth science.

Since 2012, NASA Space Apps Challenge has hosted the two-day event, which has included more than 600, 000 people from more than 190 countries. The challenge provides an opportunity for hands-on experience with NASA science, mobilizing an engaged global community of technologists and space science enthusiasts to answer questions that benefit humanity and drive exploration.

The Space Apps Challenge invites people with many different skillsets to leverage NASA’s gold-standard datasets to address real challenges facing us here on Earth and in space,” said Amy Kaminski, engagement branch chief, Science Mission Directorate, NASA Headquarters in.
