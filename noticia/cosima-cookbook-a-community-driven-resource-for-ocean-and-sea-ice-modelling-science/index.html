Texto completo

The COSIMA Cookbook is an open educational resource for analysing ocean and sea-ice model output in Jupyter notebooks. It has been developed by the Consortium for Ocean--Sea Ice Modelling in Australia as a community resource for.

It has been developed by the Consortium for Ocean--Sea Ice Modelling in Australia (COSIMA) as a community resource for researchers, students, and practitioners working with large gridded datasets, especially output from the ACCESS ocean--sea ice model configurations.

The Cookbook is organised around a gastronomy theme: ``Cooking Tutorials'' teach generic, transferable techniques for handling model output, while "Easy Recipes", "Advanced Recipes" and "Regional Specialties" are self-contained notebooks that show how to perform concrete. Along the way, the notebooks demonstrate open-source tools for the scientific analysis of large data, including Intake catalogues, Xarray, Dask, xgcm, xESMF, eofs, Parcels, matplotlib and Cartopy.

The Cookbook addresses the steep entry cost of analysing climate model output: it packages expert, often tacit, knowledge into complete, well-documented and reusable workflows, in the same medium in which researchers actually work. It is developed openly on GitHub, is published as a documentation website, and is continuously reviewed and extended by its community through pull requests and regular hackathons.

The easy access to data and analysis it provides has contributed to the international adoption of ACCESS ocean and sea-ice model configurations, and has facilitated well over 100 peer-reviewed papers and more than 20 PhD projects.
