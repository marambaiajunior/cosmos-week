Insects can convert food waste and by-products from the food industry (such as unsold ready meals or slaughter by-products) into ingredients for animal feed. Their findings show that the risk of new BSE infections is very low under the current European control system.

The findings are published in the journal Frontiers in Microbiology. In all scenarios investigated, the calculated risk of new BSE infections in cattle was very low, even under unfavorable conditions, such as accidentally feeding cattle pig feed containing insect meal.

The results provide new insights into how food waste streams can be safely used in insect rearing.
