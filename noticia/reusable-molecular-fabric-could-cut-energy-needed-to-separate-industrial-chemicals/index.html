Separating out a particular toxic chemical from industrial processes linked to the manufacture of nylon, paint, certain foams found in furniture and even detergents requires a lot of energy and hassle. The research is published in the journal Nature.

This article has been reviewed according to Science X's editorial process and policies. The new fabric is made of four molecular networks that weave above and below each other to create a layer with an interlocking pattern.

The new fabric is self-assembling, meaning that the molecular networks arrange and interconnect on their own. The whole thing converges by itself based on the underlying chemistry, which is especially cool because it allows the creation of these new materials in a facile way," Sessler said.

The fabric absorbed 97.9% of the benzene.
