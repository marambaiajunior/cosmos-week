NASA NASA experiments, including a first-of-its-kind space crop production system, will travel to the International Space Station aboard Northrop Grumman’s 25th Commercial Resupply Services mission for the agency. Investigations from NASA’s Biological and Physical Sciences Division will advance research that benefits life on Earth and future exploration, expanding knowledge of space crops and human health.

To help understand how crop production works in microgravity, NASA is launching a novel space crops experiment. The new prototype growth chamber will provide critical insights for developing safe, nutritious, and reliable crop production systems for future Moon Base habitats and Mars missions.

Knowledge gained from the space crop production system also could help inform agricultural practices such as vertical farming and indoor growing systems. This experiment will validate a miniature fluid-handling system designed to mix, deliver, and maintain the nutrient-rich liquid needed to keep the tissues alive.

Researchers could use the findings to help identify potential neurological risks associated with spaceflight and develop strategies to protect astronaut health.
