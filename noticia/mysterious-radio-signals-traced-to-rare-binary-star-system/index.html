The researchers traced the mysterious radio signals, which repeat every 8 minutes, back to the white dwarf. The signals come from the white dwarf star and repeat every 8 minutes.

Mysterious radio signals originate from unusual binary star system Astronomers say they’ve determined the source of a mysterious radio signal that repeats every eight minutes. Scientists at the International Centre for Radio Astronomy Research (ICRAR) in Australia say the unusual radio signal is originating from a binary pair of stars, where one is a white dwarf and the other is a sunlike star.

The researchers published their peer-reviewed results in Nature Astronomy on November 12, 2026. Astronomers found the first one in 2021, and about a dozen since then.

He said: This is the first time the pulses are clear enough to see the white dwarf moving in its orbit around its companion, so we know that the pulsations are coming from a region close to the white dwarf, or possibly even from the white dwarf itself. | As the radio beams from the white dwarf pass through the larger star’s atmosphere, they provide clues about its behavior, such as revealing stellar prominences.

It’s amazing that by using radio wavelengths we can detect the storms on a star over 5, 000 light-years away.
