Texto completo

The European Southern Observatory has expressed its dismay at the approval of SpaceX's satellite AI system. The system, called Starmind, calls for another 1 million satellites in Low Earth Orbit.

The FCC just approved SpaceX's application to launch a staggering one million new satellites in Low-Earth Orbit (LEO). The system is designed to avoid using energy and resources here on Earth.

In a new statement, the ESO condemns the Starmind project as a serious detriment to astronomical observations. Their technical analysis is peer-reviewed and was published in Astronomy and Astrophysics in March 2026.

This study evaluates the effect of proposed constellations, ranging from current deployments to mega-constellations and very bright reflector concepts, on direct trail losses, diffuse background, and scattered sky brightness. Conversely, mega-constellations with 10 6 satellites render trails pervasive, thereby affecting the majority of long exposures," they write.

Extremely bright satellites pose a far more severe threat: a 5000-satellite Reflect Orbital -like constellation could elevate the scattered sky background by 20, 30%, and a population of 50 000 could increase it by 200, 300%," the ESO paper states.
