Key design decisions The SR-1 Freedom spacecraft design has evolved since NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman first introduced the concept in March at the agency’s Ignition event, where leadership shared plans to develop a fission-powered spacecraft in time for the next Mars. Planned for launch in late 2028, Space Reactor-1 (SR-1) Freedom will use both nuclear and solar power to deliver SkyFall to Mars.
