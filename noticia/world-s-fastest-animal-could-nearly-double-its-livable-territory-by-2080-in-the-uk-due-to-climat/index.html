World's fastest animal could nearly double its livable territory by 2080 in the UK due to climate change

Global warming and climate change could have the unexpected bounty of making Great Britain a more welcoming home for the fastest animal on Earth.

Texto completo

Global warming and climate change could have the unexpected bounty of making Great Britain a more welcoming home for the fastest animal on Earth. The area suitable for peregrine falcons within the UK could grow by 74% by the year 2080 under a high-emissions climate scenario, a new study has shown.

Most suffered devastating declines in the mid-20th century due to the widespread use of organochlorine insecticides, which accumulated in raptors to catastrophic effect. When the EU banned these compounds from the mid-1970s onward, populations of many raptors tended to bounce back.

Nevertheless, the British Trust for Ornithology estimates that the UK's breeding population of peregrine falcons decreased by 57% between 1995 and 2024 due to illegal persecution, declines in available prey and avian flu. Panter aimed to predict changes in environmental suitability across the UK for 16 breeding raptor species under two IPCC emissions scenarios: the "middle-of-the-road" socioeconomic pathway SSP2, 4.5 and the "worst-case" SSP5, 8.5 pathway.

Input for the projections came from "niche models" fitted around 14 environmental variables, including temperature range, various measures of precipitation, elevation and the percentage of land covered by five types of habitat. The results showed that the UK area suitable for peregrine falcons could expand from 110, 257 km² (42, 600 square miles) today to 155, 000 km² (59, 800 square miles) (+40.6%) under SSP2, 4.5 by 2060, and to 191, 319 km² (73, 900 square miles) (+73.5%) under SSP5, 8.5 by 2080.

The area suitable for hen harriers could increase from 74, 835 km² (28, 900 square miles) today to 96, 025 km² (37, 100 square miles) (+28.3%) under SSP2, 4.5 by 2060, and to 105, 553 km² (40, 800 square miles) (+41.0%) under SSP5, 8.5 by 2080.
